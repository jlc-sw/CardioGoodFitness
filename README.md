# CardioGoodFitness - Exploratory Data Analysis

Exploratory analysis of CardioGoodFitness customer data using Python and pandas to examine customer characteristics, treadmill product usage and purchasing patterns.

## Analysis

The dataset contains 180 customer records across three treadmill products: TM195, TM498 and TM798.

The analysis explores:

- Customer age distribution
- Product usage by gender
- Income differences between products
- Income and expected mileage
- Fitness level and expected mileage
- Product purchase distribution

## Key Findings

- TM195 is the most frequently purchased product, followed by TM498 and TM798.
- TM798 customers show substantially higher income levels and higher expected product usage.
- Higher self-reported fitness is associated with greater expected mileage.
- The customer population is concentrated primarily among younger adults.
